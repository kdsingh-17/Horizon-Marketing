Brew & Bean Cafe Case Study

How we increased foot traffic by 45% for a local coffee shop in 3 months

Get Similar Results

The Challenge

Brew & Bean Cafe, a family-owned coffee shop in downtown, was struggling with declining foot traffic and competition from national chains. Despite having excellent coffee and service, they were invisible to the local community and new residents.

Our Strategy

1

Local SEO Optimization

Optimized Google Business Profile, local directories, and implemented geo-targeted keywords

2

Instagram & Facebook Campaigns

Daily engaging content, user-generated content campaigns, and local influencer partnerships

3

Community Events

Organized "Local Artist Nights" and "Coffee Tasting Saturdays" to build community engagement

4

Email Marketing

Weekly newsletter with exclusive offers for local residents and office workers

Results Achieved

Increase in foot traffic

156%
