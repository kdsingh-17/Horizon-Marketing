The Challenge

Main Street Boutique, a local fashion retailer, was struggling to compete with big-box stores and online retailers during the holiday season. They needed a strategy to attract local shoppers and increase seasonal revenue without massive advertising budgets.

Our Multi-Channel Strategy

1

Email Marketing Campaign

12-day holiday countdown with exclusive offers, gift guides, and abandoned cart recovery

2

Social Media Blitz

Daily Instagram Stories, Facebook Live shopping events, and user-generated content contests

3

Local Influencer Partnerships

Collaborated with 5 local fashion influencers for gift guide features and store visits

4

In-Store Experience Enhancement

Free gift wrapping, complimentary hot cocoa, and extended holiday hours promoted online
