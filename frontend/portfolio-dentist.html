The Challenge

Bright Smile Dentistry was struggling to attract new patients despite having excellent dental services. They were buried on page 3 of Google search results for key terms like "dentist near me" and "teeth cleaning [city]". Most patients were finding them through word-of-mouth only.

Our Strategy

1

Complete Google Business Profile Optimization

Professional photos, detailed services, regular posts, and review management

2

Local SEO & Citation Building

Optimized website with local keywords, built 50+ local citations, and improved NAP consistency

3

Patient Review Strategy

Automated review requests after appointments, responding to all reviews professionally

Educational Content Creation

Blog posts about dental health, video testimonials, and before/after galleries
