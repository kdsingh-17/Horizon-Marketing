Service Overview

Our Analytics & Reporting service provides you with clear, actionable insights into your marketing performance. We track every metric that matters and present it in easy-to-understand reports that show exactly how your marketing is performing and where to optimize.

What's Included:

  • Google Analytics setup and configuration
  • Custom dashboard creation for key metrics
  • Monthly performance reports with insights
  • Conversion tracking setup and optimization
  • ROI calculation and attribution modeling

  • Goal tracking and KPI monitoring
  • Regular strategy recommendations based on data
